## Sinopsis

LOCAL HEROES kicks off with three stand- alone stories that reintroduce the heroes and villains of Astro City, followed by the 2-part tale "Dark Ages." The series begins with the tale "Newcomers," introducing Pete Donacek, the genial doorman at one of Astro City's finest hotels. New to the city? Want to see the heroes? Pete knows the best places, where you're most likely to spot 'em, and the neighborhoods to avoid. But Pete was a newcomer to the city himself once - and has a story of his own to tell.
