## Sinopsis

Is Gwen Stacy's alternate Earth ready for...Gwenom?! Matt "Kingpin" Murdock's plans are coming to fruition - and if you know how he plans, you know that ain't pretty. Meanwhile, Gwen's life is about to be irrevocably changed -when she bonds with her universe's version of the Venom symbiote! What will this mean for her relationship with Murdock? With her father? With the Osborns?! As Spider-Woman descends into darkness, can she fi nd her way back to the light...or does fate only hold one end in store for Gwen?
